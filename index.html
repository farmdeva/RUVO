What is an Aqua Kart (Aquacart) and how does RUVO compare?

An Aqua Kart (also searched as aquakart, aqua cart, or aquacart) is an agile water go-kart featuring automotive-style steering wheel handling and a low-slung bucket seating cockpit inches from the water line. Unlike bulky traditional jet skis, RUVO combines pure go-kart agility with an enclosed 12 kW electric water-jet / hydro-jet drive, allowing riders to carve high-G slalom turns on lakes, backwaters, and coastal bays with immediate electric torque.

How does the 12 kW water-jet / hydro-jet propulsion work?

RUVO is driven by a submerged direct-drive water jet (hydrojet) pump. Water is drawn through an intake grate beneath the V-hull and accelerated through a high-pressure directional nozzle at up to 40 km/h. Because there is no exposed external propeller, swimmers and passengers can safely board via the aft teak platform with zero propeller strike danger, making it fully compliant with stringent eco-tourism and beach resort guidelines.

How is the battery charged, and what is the cycle life?

RUVO carries a 5.8 kWh ternary lithium marine battery pack rated for 800 charge cycles, providing 40–60 minutes of active endurance. It recharges effortlessly from any standard Indian single-phase 16A domestic 3-pin plug (AC 100V–260V) using its dedicated 2,000 W charger, taking just 4–5 hours from empty with zero three-phase industrial infrastructure required.
