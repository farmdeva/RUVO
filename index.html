Naval Architecture & Packaging.

Balancing lightweight hydrodynamics with rugged impact resilience. Every contour serves a functional marine purpose.

01

Rotomolded Polyethylene Hull

Constructed from high-density rotomolded PE. Unlike fragile fiberglass gelcoat that cracks on impact, this hull naturally absorbs groundings on sandbars and shallow rocky shores without structural damage.

02

Automotive Go-Kart Helm

Three-spoke racing steering wheel linked directly to the hydrojet vectoring nozzle. Provides responsive, tactile feedback with instant digital trigger throttle control.

03

Centerline Low-CG Battery Bay

The 5.8 kWh ternary lithium battery enclosure sits low in the keel. This low center of gravity provides exceptional self-righting stability during aggressive slalom maneuvers.

04

Integrated Aft Boarding Step

Teak-textured marine traction deck at the stern allows effortless deep-water re-entry. Because the jet impeller is safely enclosed inside the hull, there is zero propeller hazard for swimmers.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Answers to common questions from commercial water sports operators, resort owners, and private marine thrill-seekers.

What is an Aqua Kart and how does RUVO differ from traditional jet skis?

An Aqua Kart (also searched as aquakart or water go-kart) features automotive-style steering wheel handling with a low-slung bucket seat positioned inches above the waterline. Unlike bulky petrol jet skis that require body leaning, RUVO lets you carve high-G slalom turns on water with familiar steering-wheel control and direct-drive electric torque.

What is the price of the RUVO electric jet ski in India?

RUVO is priced at an introductory ₹8 Lakhs to ₹10 Lakhs (Ex-Showroom). Imported petrol personal watercraft (Sea-Doo, Yamaha WaveRunner) typically cost ₹18L to ₹28L+ in India due to high customs duties and freight. In addition, RUVO runs on standard electricity, costing just ₹35 to ₹50 per charge.

How does charging work from standard Indian domestic sockets?

RUVO carries a 5.8 kWh ternary lithium marine battery pack that charges from any standard 16A 3-pin domestic socket (AC 100V–260V) via its 2,000 W charger in 4 to 5 hours from empty. No 3-phase industrial power is required.

Why is RUVO ideal for commercial water sports operators in India?

Operators in Goa, Kerala, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat, and reservoir lakes spend hundreds of thousands annually on fuel and 2-stroke engine overhauls. RUVO eliminates fuel overhead and engine maintenance, while the rotomolded polyethylene hull withstands repeated sandy beach landings without fiberglass cracking.

Is the water jet safe for swimmers and resort guests?

Yes. RUVO utilizes a submerged direct-drive water jet pump with an enclosed internal impeller. There is no exposed external propeller, ensuring 100% swimmer safety around the aft swim platform and full compliance with eco-resort regulations.
